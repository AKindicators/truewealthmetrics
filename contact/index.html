What you can contact us about

  • A result that looks wrong: tell us the calculator, the inputs you used, and the result you expected.
  • An error or outdated fact in a guide: include the page address and the sentence or figure concerned, and a source if you have one.
  • Technical problems: such as a broken link or a layout problem on your device. A screenshot and your browser name help.
  • Ideas: calculators or guides you would find useful.

What we can’t help with

We can’t give personal financial advice or recommend specific products, and we can’t look at your accounts. For personal advice, speak to a regulated adviser. In the UK, MoneyHelper offers free, impartial guidance and can help you find one.

Please don’t email account numbers, passwords or other sensitive financial details. We don’t need them to look into a problem.

Your privacy

We use your email address and message only to reply and to fix any problem you report. See the privacy policy for how long we keep emails and your rights.
