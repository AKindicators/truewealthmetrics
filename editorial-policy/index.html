Editorial Policy & Methodology

The standards we follow when we build calculators and write guides: accuracy, transparency about assumptions, independence and prompt corrections.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Purpose

TrueWealthMetrics exists to help people understand the numbers behind everyday financial decisions. Everything we publish is general education. It is not personal financial, tax, legal or investment advice, and TrueWealthMetrics is not regulated to give advice.

Accuracy

  • Calculators use standard, documented formulas (amortisation, compound growth, annualised returns). Before a calculator is published or changed, automated tests compare it with independent reference values, including edge cases. The formula, conventions and limitations are shown on every calculator page.
  • Guides should explain concepts accurately, include worked examples that can be checked, and link to primary sources for facts that can change, such as tax allowances, interest rate data or official guidance.
  • Country-specific rules must name the country they apply to and the date they were checked. If we can’t verify a rule from an official source, we don’t present it as fact.

Sources we prefer

We prefer primary and official sources, for example:

  • UK: MoneyHelper (government-backed guidance), Financial Conduct Authority, GOV.UK, Office for National Statistics, Bank of England.
  • US: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, Investor.gov (SEC), Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Commercial sources may be cited for context, but not as the only support for a factual claim.

Publication and update dates

Guides show the date they were first published and the date they were last updated. Calculator pages show when their formulas were last reviewed. We only change these dates when the content or calculation has actually been reviewed or changed.

Independence, advertising and affiliate links

TrueWealthMetrics does not currently carry advertising or affiliate links, and no company pays to appear in our content. If we introduce advertising or affiliate partnerships in future:

  • sponsored and affiliate links will be clearly labelled and marked with appropriate link attributes;
  • advertising will never appear inside calculator inputs or results, or change how a calculator works;
  • we will not recommend a product because of a commercial relationship; and
  • this policy and the privacy policy will be updated before any change goes live.

Where we link to another website owned by the same person who runs TrueWealthMetrics, we will say so.

Corrections

If you think a calculation or statement is wrong, email support@truewealthmetrics.com with the page address and what you expected. We check every report. Confirmed errors are fixed, the page’s update date is changed, and significant corrections to a calculator are noted on the methodology page.

Expert review

Content is not currently reviewed by an independent, regulated financial adviser. See about the author for who writes and maintains the site.
