Financial disclaimer

True Wealth Metrics is a free educational financial calculator platform. This page explains what the calculators and guides can and cannot do, so you can use the results safely.

Last updated 12 November 2026

General education, not advice

Everything on True Wealth Metrics, including every calculator, chart, example and guide, is general information for education and planning. It is not financial, investment, mortgage, pension, debt, tax or legal advice. It is not a personal recommendation to buy, sell, hold, borrow, repay, transfer or switch anything.

The calculators do not know your full circumstances, and we do not ask for them. A result tells you what a set of numbers produces under the stated assumptions. It does not tell you what you should do.

We are not authorised or regulated by the FCA

True Wealth Metrics is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). It does not carry out regulated activities such as advising on investments, arranging or advising on mortgages, credit broking or debt counselling, and it does not provide any financial products. The content is not reviewed by an independent, regulated financial adviser.

You can check whether a firm or adviser is authorised on the FCA Financial Services Register.

Results are estimates

  • Every result is an estimate based on the numbers you enter and the assumptions listed with it, such as interest rate, payment timing, compounding, fees and inflation.
  • Real outcomes can differ. Interest rates change, investment returns go up and down, lenders calculate interest in different ways, and fees, charges and tax may apply.
  • Investment and pension projections are illustrations, not forecasts or promises. The value of investments can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you put in.
  • Figures quoted by a lender, provider or adviser for a specific product take priority over any estimate here.

How each calculator works, including its formula and limitations, is explained on the page itself and in the calculator methodology.

Country-specific rules

Most calculators use general maths that applies anywhere. Tax allowances, pension rules, benefits and borrowing rules differ between countries and change over time. Where a guide mentions such a rule, check the current position with an official source such as GOV.UK before you rely on it.

Where to get free, impartial help (UK)

  • MoneyHelper: free, impartial money and pensions guidance, backed by the government.
  • Free debt advice: MoneyHelper lists free, confidential debt advice services.
  • For personal advice on investments, pensions or mortgages, use a firm or adviser that is authorised by the FCA.

Your decisions

You are responsible for decisions you make using the calculators and guides. To the extent permitted by law, True Wealth Metrics is not liable for losses arising from reliance on estimates or general information on this website. Nothing here limits any liability that cannot be limited by law. See the terms of use for the full terms.
