About TrueWealthMetrics

True Wealth Metrics is a free educational financial calculator platform. It publishes personal finance calculators and plain-English guides, so you can test a money decision with your own numbers before you commit to it.

Last updated 12 November 2026

What we do

We build calculators for the everyday money questions people actually face: what is left of my pay each month, how long will this loan take to clear, how much will fees cost me, am I saving enough for retirement? Each tool shows its result, the assumptions behind it, the formula it uses and what it leaves out.

The calculators cover four areas:

  • Budgeting and saving: the budget planner and savings goal calculator.
  • Loans and debt: loan repayments, mortgages, credit card payoff and snowball vs avalanche.
  • Investing: compound interest, investment growth, fee impact and ROI.
  • Retirement: the retirement planning calculator.

The guides explain the ideas behind the numbers, such as how loan interest is charged, why fees compound and what the 4% rule does and does not tell you.

How the calculators are built

  • One calculation engine. All tools share the same, openly documented calculation code, so the same inputs give the same answer everywhere on the site.
  • Tested against independent references. Results are checked by automated tests against closed-form formulas and published textbook values, including edge cases such as 0% interest, payments that never clear a debt and goals that cannot be reached.
  • Assumptions alongside results. Each results panel lists the assumptions used, and each calculator page explains its formula and limitations.
  • Private by design. Calculations run in your browser. You don’t need an account, and nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Full details are in the calculator methodology.
