Personal Finance Author

Who writes and maintains TrueWealthMetrics, how the content is produced, and what kind of review it has.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is behind TrueWealthMetrics

TrueWealthMetrics is created and maintained by its founder, Mudasar Aziz. Mudasar builds the calculators, writes and updates the guides, and handles corrections and reader feedback.

Guides are published under the True Wealth Metrics name. Each guide shows when it was first published and when it was last updated.

Review and qualifications

We want to be clear about what kind of review the content has:

  • Calculators are checked by automated tests against independent reference calculations before they are published or changed. See the calculator methodology.
  • Guides are checked against primary sources such as government, regulator and official statistics websites where these exist.
  • TrueWealthMetrics content is not currently reviewed by an independent, regulated financial adviser. If that changes, the reviewer will be named on each page they review, with credentials you can verify.

Nothing on this site is personal financial advice. TrueWealthMetrics is not authorised or regulated to give financial advice.

Approach to writing about money

  • Plain English. Explain the idea, show a worked example and say what changes the answer.
  • Show the assumptions. Every estimate depends on rates, timing and what is left out, and we say so.
  • Say which country a rule applies to. Tax, pension and borrowing rules differ between countries. Where a guide relies on a country-specific rule, it should say so and link to the official source.
  • No promises. We do not present projected investment returns as guaranteed, and we avoid exaggerated claims.
