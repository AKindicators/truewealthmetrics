Privacy Policy

How True Wealth Metrics handles personal information. In short: calculator inputs never leave your device, there are no accounts, and the site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Summary

  • Calculator inputs stay on your device. Calculations run in your web browser. What you type is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is not stored.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. The site does not set cookies, does not use your browser’s local storage, and has no analytics, advertising or social media scripts.
  • Our hosting provider processes basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to fix any problem you report.

Who is responsible for your information

True Wealth Metrics is a trading name. The website is run by [PENDING owner confirmation: operator], a sole trader based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of personal information collected through it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact for any privacy question or request: truewealthmetrics@gmail.com.

Calculator data

All calculators run locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your inputs and results are not sent to True Wealth Metrics or to anyone else, and they are not saved when you leave or reload the page. If you choose to download a CSV file or print your results, that copy is created on your own device. No calculator asks for your name, contact details, account numbers or other identifying information.

Hosting and security

The website is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. When you visit any website, your device sends technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. Cloudflare processes this information on our behalf to deliver pages and to protect the site from attacks and abuse.

  • Purpose and lawful basis: running a secure, working website. This is in our legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and in yours as a visitor.
  • Retention: we do not keep our own visitor logs. Cloudflare keeps limited technical logs for its own short periods, as described in its privacy policy.
  • Email protection: Cloudflare hides our email address from automated spam harvesters. This uses a small script served from our own website; it does not set cookies or track you.

Cookies and similar technologies

True Wealth Metrics does not set cookies and does not use local storage, session storage or similar technologies to store information on your device. We checked this on every type of page.

Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in unusual situations, for example if your connection looks automated and needs to be checked. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, strictly necessary cookies do not need consent.

If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy first, and we will ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used.

If you email us

When you email truewealthmetrics@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write.

  • Purpose: to reply to you and to fix any problem or error you report.
  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and keeping the site accurate (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Where it is stored: our contact address is a Gmail account, so messages are received and stored by Google as our email service provider.
  • How long we keep it: [PENDING owner confirmation: retention]
  • We do not add you to a mailing list, sell your details or share them with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Please do not email account numbers, passwords or other sensitive financial details. We never need them.

Who else handles personal information

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting, delivery and security.
  • Google (Gmail): receiving and storing email sent to our contact address.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

International transfers

Our service providers are based in, or use servers in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by the providers’ legal safeguards, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the ICO’s International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses. You can find details in each provider’s privacy and data processing terms.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it; and
  • object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests.

To make a request, email truewealthmetrics@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. There is normally no charge.

No decisions about you are made by automated means, and no profiling takes place.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

The website is general education for adults. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other websites

Guides link to other websites, such as government and regulator resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the way we handle information changes, and change the date at the top.
