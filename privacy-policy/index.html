Privacy Policy

What information TrueWealthMetrics does and does not collect. In short: calculator inputs never leave your device, there are no accounts, and we do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Summary

  • Calculator inputs stay on your device. Calculations run in your browser. What you type is not sent to us, stored on our servers or shared with anyone.
  • No accounts. You can use every calculator without signing up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are used on this site at present.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by TrueWealthMetrics. For any privacy question or request, contact support@truewealthmetrics.com.

Calculator data

All calculators are processed locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your inputs and results are not transmitted to TrueWealthMetrics or to third parties. They are not saved after you leave the page, unless you choose to download a CSV file or print the results yourself. You do not need to enter your name, account numbers or other identifying details to use any calculator.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information needed to deliver web pages and protect the site from abuse, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some circumstances, for example to distinguish people from automated traffic. This processing is necessary to run a secure website. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Cookies

TrueWealthMetrics does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy first, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies are set.

If you contact us

When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond and to fix any problem you report. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Links to other websites

Guides may link to other websites, such as government and regulator resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct or delete it. In the UK and the EU these rights come from the UK GDPR and the GDPR. To make a request, email support@truewealthmetrics.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if how we handle information changes, and update the date at the top.
